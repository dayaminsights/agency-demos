The Space

We didn't build a theme.
We found a real one.

Harvest & Co sits inside Dubai Garden Centre — a genuinely green, plant-filled space that makes the city feel very far away the moment you walk in. Surrounded by living plants, natural light, and the quiet hum of something good being cooked, this is a place where the setting changes how a meal feels.

Sheikh Zayed Road is right there. It just doesn't feel like it. We build our menu around ingredients that are fresh, seasonal, and cooked to order — not assembled from a cold chain. The result is food that tastes the way it should in a room that looks and smells exactly right.
