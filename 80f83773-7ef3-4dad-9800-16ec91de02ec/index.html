An Indore Institution

Part of 56 Dukan Since Before You Were Born

Chappan Dukan — 56 Dukan — is Indore's most famous food street, and Johny Hot Dog has been its neighbour for longer than most visitors have been alive. One local puts it simply: eating here for the past 30 years, returning every visit back to the city. That's not loyalty to a restaurant; that's loyalty to a part of Indore itself.

For out-of-towners who've heard about it: this is a real Indore institution. Not a chain, not a newcomer — the original counter that proved a single brilliant item, priced right and made consistently, is all it takes.
