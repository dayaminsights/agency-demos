The Manifesto

One dish.
Done right.

Khao Man Gai is built around one dish done right: Thai chicken rice the way it's eaten in Bangkok — poached low and slow for that silky pull, or fried to a shatter-crisp finish, always on fragrant jasmine rice with the broth it was cooked in.

We brought the full picture to Melbourne's CBD — the sauces, the soup, the sticky rice, the milk tea — nothing stripped back for Western comfort. One dish, every component at its standard. That's the whole point.
