Questions patients ask most

Is dental implant treatment painful? +
Most patients feel little to no discomfort during implant placement because we administer precise local anaesthesia before we begin. Post-procedure soreness is typically mild and managed with standard pain relief. Dr. Kanungo will walk you through exactly what to expect at your consultation.
→ Call us to ask anything before you commit
How long does a full implant take? +
The timeline depends on bone density and whether any preparatory work is needed, but most patients complete the full process — consultation, placement, healing, and crown fitting — within three to six months. Dr. Kanungo will give you a personalised timeline at your first visit.
→ Book a consultation to get your timeline
