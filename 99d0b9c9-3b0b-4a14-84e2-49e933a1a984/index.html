Our Story

Not Mediterranean-Inspired.
Actually Greek.

"Tired of 'Mediterranean-inspired' menus that have nothing to do with Greece — and everything to do with playing it safe?"

Plaka is named after the oldest neighbourhood in Athens — narrow streets, neighbourhood tables, food that doesn't need explaining. We brought that same unhurried energy to Dubai: a menu rooted in Greek café culture, drinks you won't find anywhere else on this side of the city, and a room that actually makes you want to stay. No theatre. Just good food at prices that don't punish you for coming back.
