Our Story

The Address Pune Gives Directions By

We are Cafe Goodluck — the address Pune's Deccan Gymkhana neighbourhood has given directions by for generations. Goodluck Chowk is not just where we sit; it is a landmark so embedded in the city's mental map that Punekars navigate by it the way other cities navigate by monuments.

The Irani cafe tradition that shaped Deccan's mornings — strong chai, fresh-baked mawa cake, generous bun maska — is the lineage this address belongs to. The keema pav and bun maska you remember are still made the same way.

This Ferguson College Rd location carries that same kitchen and those same recipes. This is where you find us now — and this site is our first official home on the internet, built to put accurate menus, real hours, and our own ordering system back in our hands.
